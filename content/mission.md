# Our Mission

## The Problem

We live in a country that fosters and rewards profit above everything else. There is little to do about this from a democratic point of view. Our government is bought and paid for with two goals in mind: profit or power. That is America.

Corruption runs deeper than we can imagine, and it's why these two goals will never change. War is profit and power. To become emperor of Rome, you needed a big army. To win favor with their people, you needed to bring back the spoils of war.

It goes deeper than the US.

## Our Response

That is why we are building a company with political aims in mind from day one. We aim to revolutionize capitalism within the clothing industry.

We're not here to play the game better. We're here to change the rules.

## Our Commitments

### 1. Radical Transparency

Our company is fully transparent with all money we earn and spend. You can see exactly where your dollar goes.

We publish quarterly financial reports that show:
- Total revenue
- Material costs
- Employee compensation (total)
- Advertising spend
- Operational expenses
- Employee events and culture
- Savings and reserves
- Profit distribution

We won't hide behind vague "operating costs." If we spent money, you'll know on what.

### 2. Equal Pay. No Exceptions.

Every employee—from the person who answers your emails to the person who designed the clothes you're wearing—earns the same base salary: **$70,000/year**.

Not "competitive." Not "based on experience." Equal.

Every employee receives the best possible health insurance and benefits. The same plan. The same coverage. No tiers.

### 3. 100% Profit Sharing

After all expenses are paid, here's what happens to profit:

- **25%** goes into savings and future investments (so we can keep doing this)
- **75%** is distributed equally among all employees

Not executives. Not shareholders. Employees. The people who actually do the work.

Distributions happen quarterly. Everyone sees the numbers. Everyone shares equally.

## Why This Matters

Most companies exist to extract value. They take your labor, your creativity, your time—and they give the profits to people who already have more than they need.

We believe a company can exist to distribute value instead.

When you buy from us, you're not making a CEO richer. You're funding a different way of doing business. You're voting with your wallet for a world where work is rewarded fairly.

## The Revolution Isn't a Metaphor

We called this company Revolution for a reason.

We're not interested in being a "ethical brand" that pays lip service to values while operating the same way everyone else does. We're not interested in greenwashing or performative activism.

We are building proof that another way is possible.

Every shirt we sell. Every person we hire. Every dollar we share.

This is the revolution.
